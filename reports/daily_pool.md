## Data rights: owner to accept before launch

The starting fives come from games started in the Kaggle dataset `sumitrodatta/nba-aba-baa-stats` ("NBA Stats (1947-present)"), which is **scraped from Basketball-Reference**. Sports Reference's terms of use forbid scraping its sites and any public or commercial use of the data without its written permission. Kaggle's CC0 label is the uploader's and does not clear Sports Reference's rights.

- `teams.json` publishes no Basketball-Reference number. The choice of each five and the spelling of the names (with accents) come from it; PPG and signature stats come from the existing `PlayerStatistics.csv`.
- This joins the existing commercial-data question (`docs/product/HANDOFF.md`, "Commercial-data gate", F00). It is stricter: the terms cover public use, not only commercial use, and Daily Three is public.
- The owner must accept this, or get permission, before Daily Three launches (Session 5).

## Who started? Games started (Q2)

Each team's five is the 5 players with the most **games started** (GS) for it that season in Basketball-Reference's season totals (`data/raw/bbref/Player Totals.csv`). Ties go to minutes, a player needs 20 games for the team, and a traded player counts only their rows for this team, never the 2TM/3TM season totals. GS is recorded for every season in the pool, 1985–86 to 2025–26.

- **Teams** map to Basketball-Reference abbreviations by era-correct city and name (`Team Abbrev.csv`), or by nickname alone where the city differs (the 2005–06 and 2006–07 Oklahoma City Hornets, the 2025–26 LA Clippers). All 1177 team-seasons map to exactly one.
- **Players** match `PlayerStatistics.csv` within the team-season by name: accents and punctuation dropped, Jr./Sr./II–IV ignored, then 39 aliases in `NAME_ALIASES` for nicknames and other spellings ("Nenê" / "Nene Hilario", "Fat Lever" / "Lafayette Lever", "Yi Jianlian" / "Jianlian Yi"). Every starter must match exactly one player, or the script stops.
- **Unmatched players** among each team's top 8 by GS, over all 1177 team-seasons: none. They are missing from `PlayerStatistics.csv`, so they cannot carry stats.

## Cross-checks: the earlier rules against games started

The first version of this PR picked fives with three rules, each season taking the first it supported. They now only check games started:

- **Starter flag**: `PlayerStatistics.csv`'s `startingPosition`, set for exactly 5 players a team-game only from 2017–18 on, and not in 2021–22 (from 1996–97 to 2016–17 it is filled for about 9 players a team-game; before that and in 2021–22 it is blank).
- **Bench-points inference**: in each game the starters' points must add up to the team's score less its bench points (`TeamStatisticsExtended.csv`, usable from 2003–04; from 1996–97 to 2002–03 its `benchPoints` holds the whole team score).
- **Minutes proxy**: the 5 highest-minute players in each game stand in for the starters.

## Data notes

- **Missing games.** 4 team-seasons have up to 2 fewer games in the player file than `index.json` counts, 0 of them in the pool. Per-game stats use the games the player file has.
- **Names** use Basketball-Reference's spelling, in UTF-8 ("J.R. Smith", "Toni Kukoč"). Pool starters with non-ASCII letters: Bogdan Bogdanović, Bojan Bogdanović, Donatas Motiejūnas, Hedo Türkoğlu, Jonas Valančiūnas, José Calderón, Jusuf Nurkić, Kristaps Porziņģis, Luka Dončić, Manu Ginóbili, Nenad Krstić, Nenê, Nikola Jokić, Peja Stojaković, Rasho Nesterović, Sasha Danilović, Toni Kukoč, Vladimir Radmanović.
- **Regular season** is every game whose id starts with 2, including NBA Cup group and knockout games (they count in the standings) and not the Cup final. About half of 2000–01's player rows have no team id; they are matched by team name.
- **Signature stats are league-relative.** A 1980s guard's 0.3 threes a game can clear the 80% bar because few players shot threes then.
